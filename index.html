Eliminate the "Human Robots" in enterprises

By “human robots,” we mean roles that can be defined simply as:

/ The Question
Why weren't these eliminated in the information age?

Because the “when” often demanded professional judgment,
while the “then” required specialized knowledge and flexibility.
